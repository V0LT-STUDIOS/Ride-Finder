A NOTE ON STREET RIDING

TIRES ≠
ROAD LEGAL.

Many e-motos can physically ride on pavement with appropriate tires, but tire choice does not make an off-road bike legal on public roads. Legal use can depend on registration, equipment, insurance, licensing, vehicle classification, rider age and local rules.

RideFinder distinguishes pavement-capable from street-registrable. Always verify the exact model, variant and laws where you live.
